Privacy Policy — E-Earning-King-YT

Last updated: 26 November 2026

E-Earning-King-YT is a small personal tool that uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is used by one person, for their own channel only.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own YouTube channel data: channel details, video titles, descriptions, upload status, and video files the owner chooses to upload. It does not access any other person's data.

How data is stored

Authorisation tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine, in an encrypted file. No data is sent to any third party, and no data is sold or shared.

Third-party services

The app uses Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, which are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
